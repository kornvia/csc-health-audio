1Find where to pay找缴费处

  1. qǐng请wèn问zài在nǎ哪lǐ里jiǎo缴fèi费 ？

    You sayWhere do I pay, please?

  2. kě可yǐ以zài在zì自zhù助jī机shang上sǎo扫mǎ码jiǎo缴fèi费 。

    CashierYou can scan to pay at the kiosk.

2Ask how to pay问付款方式

  1. néng能shuā刷wài外kǎ卡ma吗 ？

    You sayCan I use a foreign card?

  2. zhè这tái台bù不shōu收wài外kǎ卡 ，chuāng窗kǒu口kě可yǐ以fù付xiàn现jīn金 。

    CashierThis machine won’t take foreign cards. You can pay cash at the counter.

3Ask about insurance问保险直付

  1. wǒ我de的bǎo保xiǎn险néng能zhí直fù付ma吗 ？

    You sayCan you bill my insurer directly?

  2. nín您xiān先fù付 ，zài再zhǎo找bǎo保xiǎn险gōng公sī司bào报xiāo销 。

    CashierYou pay first, then claim it back from your insurer.

4Get your receipts要发票和清单

  1. qǐng请gěi给wǒ我fā发piào票hé和fèi费yòng用qīng清dān单 。

    You sayAn invoice and an itemised bill, please.

  2. fā发piào票shì是diàn电zǐ子de的 ，qīng清dān单gěi给nín您 。

    CashierThe invoice is electronic. Here’s the itemised bill.

5Ask for medical documents要病历和证明

  1. zài在nǎ哪r儿fù复yìn印bìng病lì历 、kāi开zhěn诊duàn断zhèng证míng明 ？

    You sayWhere can I get copies of my records and a diagnosis certificate?

  2. fù复yìn印bìng病lì历qù去bìng病àn案shì室 ，zhěn诊duàn断zhèng证míng明zhǎo找yī医shēng生kāi开 。

    CashierCopies at the records office; ask your doctor for a diagnosis certificate.
