At the dentist

看牙

Tap any line to hear it. Play all runs the 10 lines in order.

1Point to the tooth说牙疼

  1. wǒ我zhè这kē颗yá牙téng疼 ，yǐ以qián前bǔ补de的diào掉le了 。

    You sayThis tooth hurts. An old filling fell out.

  2. lái来 ，zhāng张zuǐ嘴 ，wǒ我kàn看yí一xià下 。

    DentistOpen wide and let me take a look.

2Hear about the X-ray拍牙片

  1. xiān先qù去pāi拍zhāng张piàn片zi子kàn看kan看 。

    DentistLet’s take a dental X-ray first.

  2. zài在nǎ哪r儿pāi拍yá牙piàn片 ？

    You sayWhere do I get the X-ray?

3Ask about options问方案和费用

  1. yǒu有nǎ哪xiē些fāng方àn案 ？gè各yào要duō多shao少qián钱 ？

    You sayWhat are my options? How much is each?

  2. yǒu有liǎng两ge个fāng方àn案 ，wǒ我gěi给nín您jiǎng讲yí一xià下 。

    DentistThere are two options. I’ll explain them.

4Say what you can feel问麻药、说感觉

  1. dǎ打le了má麻yào药 ，xiàn现zài在hái还yǒu有gǎn感jué觉ma吗 ？

    DentistWe’ve numbed the area. Can you still feel it?

  2. hái还yǒu有gǎn感jué觉 ，yǒu有diǎn点téng疼 。

    You sayI can still feel it. It hurts a bit.

5Ask about aftercare问术后注意

  1. zuò做wán完yǐ以hòu后 ，duō多jiǔ久néng能chī吃dōng东xi西 ？

    You sayAfter this, how long until I can eat?

  2. huí回qù去zhào照zhè这zhāng张dān单zi子zuò做 。

    DentistFollow this sheet when you get home.
