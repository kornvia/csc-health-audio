Telling the doctor

1Point to the pain哪里疼

  1. nǐ你nǎ哪r儿bù不shū舒fu服 ？

    DoctorWhat’s bothering you?

  2. wǒ我dù肚zi子téng疼 。

    You sayMy stomach hurts.

2Say when it started疼了多久

    DoctorHow long has it hurt?

    You sayIt’s been hurting for two days.

  2. shì是zhàng胀tòng痛 ，liù六fēn分 。
