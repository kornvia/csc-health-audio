Getting your medicine

取药、用法

Tap any line to hear it. Play all runs the 10 lines in order.

1Collect your medicine窗口取药

  1. wǒ我lái来qǔ取yào药 。

    You sayI’m here for my medicine.

  2. qǔ取yào药dān单gěi给wǒ我kàn看yí一xià下 。

    PharmacistShow me your pickup slip, please.

2Ask how much and how often问用法用量

  1. yì一tiān天jǐ几cì次 ？měi每cì次yòng用duō多shao少 ？

    You sayHow often? How much each time?

  2. yào药dài袋shang上xiě写zhe着yì一tiān天jǐ几cì次 、měi每cì次yòng用duō多shao少 。

    PharmacistThe bag says how often and how much.

3Before or after meals?饭前还是饭后

  1. fàn饭qián前chī吃hái还shi是fàn饭hòu后chī吃 ？

    You sayBefore or after meals?

  2. zhè这yào药fàn饭hòu后chī吃 。

    PharmacistFor this medicine, after meals.

4Ask about alcohol or other medicine问注意事项

  1. néng能hē喝jiǔ酒ma吗 ？néng能hé和bié别de的yào药yì一qǐ起chī吃ma吗 ？

    You sayCan I drink alcohol? Can I take it with my other medicines?

  2. bǎ把nín您zài在chī吃de的yào药gěi给wǒ我kàn看kan看 。

    PharmacistLet me see the medicines you’re taking.

5Ask how long问吃几天

  1. yào要chī吃jǐ几tiān天 ？chī吃wán完le了ne呢 ？

    You sayHow many days? What do I do when I finish it?

  2. chī吃jǐ几tiān天xiě写zài在yào药dài袋shang上 ，xù续yào药tí提qián前wèn问yī医shēng生 。

    PharmacistHow many days is on the bag. If you’ll need more, ask your doctor early.
